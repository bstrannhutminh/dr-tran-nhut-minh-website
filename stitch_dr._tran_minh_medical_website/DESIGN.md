## Brand & Style

### Brand Personality & Philosophy
This design system embodies high-level clinical expertise, institutional trust, and humanistic medical care tailored specifically for personal branding in musculoskeletal health, regenerative therapy, and proactive wellness. The interface balances authoritative scientific rigor with welcoming, accessible warmth. Every design choice communicates diagnostic precision, surgical reliability, and emphatic patient reassurance.

### Target Audience & Psychology
- **Primary Demographic:** Middle-aged to elderly individuals (aged 35 to 80+), along with their adult children seeking specialist care for chronic joint, bone, and systemic conditions.
- **Cognitive & Visual Needs:** High legibility, distinct contrast, unhurried pacing, generous touch targets, clear wayfinding, and an atmosphere free from visual clutter or cognitive strain.
- **Emotional Resonance:** Relief, unwavering safety, institutional credibility, and proactive hope.

### Aesthetic Movement
**Corporate Modern Clinical Elegance with Subtle Tactile Depth.** 
The aesthetic pairs structural clarity with clean architectural geometry: pristine clinical surfaces (`#F7F9FB`), deep authoritative foundations (`#002C43`), refreshing therapeutic cyan tones (`#4CA6C6`), and intentional, high-urgency accents (`#D71F17`). Visual layers rely on soft ambient light rather than harsh structural borders, creating an environment akin to a world-class private medical suite.

## Colors

### Palette Architecture & Semantic Roles
- **Primary Navy Blue (`#002C43`):** The institutional backbone. Used for deep clinical branding, major navigational headers, structural containers, high-order cards, and foundational trust elements.
- **Secondary Medical Blue (`#4CA6C6`):** The clinical healing tone. Used for clinical sub-headers, interactive badges, active tab indicators, medical specialty badges, and soft highlights.
- **Brand Accent Medical Red (`#D71F17`):** The focal priority marker. Dedicated strictly to critical action points: the primary conversion CTA ("Đặt lịch khám"), doctor branding marks ("TM+"), urgent clinical notices, and vital trust anchors. Never overused to prevent alarm.
- **Highlight Warm Orange (`#F99621`):** The human warmth accent. Used sparingly for verified accolades, doctor awards, patient rating stars, and subtle cross-symbol accents.
- **Main Text Navy (`#172B3A`):** Delivers WCAG AAA contrast against light backgrounds while remaining softer than absolute pure black.
- **Secondary Slate Gray (`#61717C`):** Subordinate metadata, consultation hours, diagnostic descriptions, and form helper text.
- **Clinical Neutral Surface (`#FFFFFF`):** High-cleanliness cards, elevated panels, and active input backgrounds.
- **Soft White Canvas (`#F7F9FB`):** Calming, clinical-grade overall background canvas that prevents eye fatigue.
- **Border Subtle (`#E4EAF0`):** Soft architectural boundary line preventing visual harshness.

## Typography

### Senior & Middle-Age Accessibility Rules
- **Base Body Sizing:** Body copy strictly starts at 16px, with 18px (`body-lg`) prioritized for long clinical narrative sections, condition explanations, and doctor notes to serve mature eyes without pinch-to-zoom.
- **Line Heights:** Set between 1.5x and 1.65x for body text to maintain clear tracking across wide lines, reducing visual re-reading fatigue.
- **Letter Spacing:** Mild negative tracking applied on bold, large headlines for commanding punchiness; neutral to slight positive tracking applied on labels and small metadata for uncompromised legibility.
- **Weights:** Restricted to 400 (Regular), 600 (Semi-bold), 700 (Bold), and 800 (Extra-bold). Ultra-thin and light weights (100–300) are forbidden to ensure robust legibility on any mobile screen.

## Layout & Spacing

### Layout Architecture
- **Grid Structure:** 12-column layout on Desktop (`max-width: 1280px` centered), 8 columns on Tablet (`768px - 1024px`), and 4 columns on Mobile (`< 768px`).
- **Clinical Rhythm:** Generous vertical sections separated by `space-2xl` to `space-3xl` to avoid informational panic and give older readers breathing room between distinct treatment offerings.
- **Touch Ergonomics:** Form fields, interactive buttons, appointment selectors, and bottom navigation ribbons must enforce a minimum hit target of `48px` vertically (ideal: `52px` - `56px`).

## Elevation & Depth

### Ambient Medical Elevation (Soft Luxury Shadows)
Rather than harsh drop shadows or flat brutalist boundaries, depth is established through diffused, multi-layered tinted shadows inspired by sterile ambient lighting in high-end medical suites:

- **Level 0 (Flat Ground):** Background `#F7F9FB`, no shadow.
- **Level 1 (Card & Specialty Containers):** 
  `box-shadow: 0 4px 20px -2px rgba(0, 44, 67, 0.05), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(0, 44, 67, 0.03);`
  Boundary: `1px solid #E4EAF0`.
- **Level 2 (Hover State & Interactive Clinical Cards):** 
  `box-shadow: 0 12px 32px -4px rgba(0, 44, 67, 0.08), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(76, 166, 198, 0.06);`
  Translates `-2px` on hover.
- **Level 3 (Booking Overlays, Modals, Quick Consultation Bar):** 
  `box-shadow: 0 24px 48px -8px rgba(0, 44, 67, 0.14), 0 8px 16px -4px rgba(0, 44, 67, 0.04);`
- **Focus Ring Elevation:** Standardized accessibility halo: `0 0 0 3px rgba(76, 166, 198, 0.35)`.

## Shapes

### Shape Semantics
- **Cards & Primary Modules:** 16px (`1rem`) to 20px (`1.25rem`) rounded corners create an approachable, high-trust sanctuary aesthetic without feeling overly whimsical.
- **Buttons & Interactive Badges:** 8px (`0.5rem`) for compact utility elements; 12px (`0.75rem`) for standard buttons; fully rounded (`9999px`) reserved specifically for specialty pill tags, category markers, and status chips.
- **Image Treatment:** Treatment photos, anatomical diagnostic renders, and doctor portraits utilize 16px–20px radii, occasionally coupled with subtle circular motif geometry echoing cell structures and joint mechanics.
